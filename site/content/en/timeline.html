Editing in the Timeline

The Timeline holds your picture on video tracks (V1, V2 …) and your sound on audio tracks (A1, A2 …). You only need a handful of tools day to day: cutting, deleting, rippling, and separating picture from sound cover most edits.

  • Premiere 27.0
  • Updated 27 Nov 2026
  • Beginner
  • Based on Adobe Help Center

Good to know: since 26.3, a Timeline preference controls whether Premiere adds tracks automatically when you edit clips into a sequence. If tracks keep multiplying, check that setting.

The tools you'll use most

ToolKeyWhat it does
SelectionVSelect, drag and move clips. You'll be on this most of the time.
RazorCCuts a clip in two where you click. Hold Shift to cut every track at once.
Ripple EditBDrag a clip edge to shorten or lengthen it; later clips move with it, so no gap appears.
Rolling EditNMoves the cut point between two clips without changing the total length.
Rate StretchRDrag a clip edge to change its speed so it fits a length.
Track Select ForwardASelects every clip to the right of where you click, for moving a whole block.

The basics

  1. Cut a clip

    Click with the Razor where you want the cut, or park the playhead and press Ctrl+K / Cmd+K (Add Edit) to cut the targeted tracks at the playhead.

  2. Delete, or ripple delete

    Select a clip and press Delete to leave a gap. To have the following clips slide over and close it, use Edit › Ripple Delete (Shift+Delete on Windows, Shift+Forward Delete on Mac), or right-click the gap and choose Ripple Delete.

  3. Trim heads and tails fast

    Park the playhead, then press Q to remove the part of the clip before it, or W to remove the part after it. The rest closes up automatically.

  4. Separate and rejoin picture and sound

    Camera clips have linked picture and sound. Select a clip and press Ctrl+L / Cmd+L to unlink or relink them. Hold Alt / Option while clicking to unlink just for that action.

  5. Make J-cuts and L-cuts

    Let the next shot's sound start before its picture (J-cut), or let the current shot's sound carry on over the new picture (L-cut). Hold Alt / Option and drag only the audio edge. Edits feel much smoother.

  6. Change speed

    Right-click a clip and choose Speed/Duration (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R), then enter a percentage. Tick Ripple Edit, Shifting Trailing Clips to avoid leaving a gap.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Tiny black gaps between clipsLeft by plain Delete. Keep snapping on and ripple delete the gaps.
  • Picture and sound out of syncCaused by dragging one without the other while unlinked. Premiere shows red numbers on the clip with the offset; right-click them to pull it back into sync.
  • The Razor cut only one trackThe clips weren't linked. Hold Shift with the Razor to cut all tracks.
  • Clips land on the wrong trackCheck track targeting and source patching on the left of the timeline.
  • Forgetting to switch back to SelectionYou stay on the Razor and slice a clip by accident. Press V after every cut.

Using a version older than 27.0

Premiere 25.x–26.x
