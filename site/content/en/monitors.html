Source Monitor vs Program Monitor

The Source Monitor is where you watch raw clips one at a time and choose the part you want. The Program Monitor shows the sequence you are building. Think of the left screen as your ingredients and the right screen as what the audience will see.

  • Premiere 27.0
  • Updated 27 Nov 2026
  • Beginner
  • Based on Adobe Help Center

Changed in 27.0: Clip Match and the clip Comparison Mode in Edit-mode monitoring have been removed. Comparing shots for colour now happens in Color Mode.

Both monitors have an Export Frame button for saving a still from the current frame.

Steps: from Source to timeline

  1. Open a clip in the Source Monitor

    Double-click a clip in the Project panel. It opens in the Source Monitor without being added to the sequence.

  2. Mark the part you want

    Play to the start, press I for an In point, play to the end and press O for an Out point. Everything between them is what gets placed.

  3. Choose picture, sound or both

    To take only one, drag the Drag video or Drag audio icon under the monitor into the timeline.

  4. Park the playhead in the timeline

    Move the playhead to where the clip should go, and check that the right tracks are targeted.

  5. Press Insert or Overwrite

    • Insert , adds the clip at the playhead and pushes everything after it to the right. The sequence gets longer.
    • Overwrite . lays the clip over what is already there. The sequence doesn't grow if there was material under it.

  6. Review and trim in the Program Monitor

    Set In and Out in the Program Monitor, then press Lift ; to remove the range and leave a gap, or Extract ' to remove it and close the gap.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Overwrite wiped out a clipOverwrite really does replace what is there. Undo with Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z and use Insert if you meant to add.
  • Insert knocked audio out of syncTracks with Sync Lock turned off don't shift with the insert. Turn Sync Lock on for every track that should move together.
  • The clip landed on the wrong trackCheck source patching and track targeting on the left of the timeline.
  • The Program Monitor looks soft, so the file seems brokenIts Resolution may be set to 1/2 or 1/4 for smoother playback. Your export is not affected.
  • A marker went to the wrong placeMarkers added in the Source Monitor stick to the clip. For a marker on the timeline, add it in the Program Monitor or the timeline.

Using a version older than 27.0

Premiere 26.x and earlier
  • The Program Monitor still has Comparison mode and Clip Match for comparing colour between shots. 27.0 moved that job to Color Mode.
  • Insert, Overwrite, Lift and Extract buttons and shortcuts work the same.
