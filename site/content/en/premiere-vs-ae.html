Premiere vs After Effects

Premiere is for editing: arranging long footage into a story, with sound, captions and colour. After Effects is for motion graphics and visual effects: detailed work on short shots. Most projects start in Premiere and send a few shots to After Effects.

  • Premiere & After Effects 27.0
  • Updated 27 Nov 2026
  • Beginner
  • Based on Adobe Help Center

Rule of thumb: minutes of footage with many clips belongs in Premiere. A few seconds where everything has to move just right belongs in After Effects.

Using both together

  1. Dynamic Link: send a clip to After Effects

    In Premiere, right-click a clip in the timeline and choose Replace With After Effects Composition. After Effects opens with a new composition containing that clip. Changes you make there show up in Premiere without rendering.

    Premiere and After Effects must be the same version, or Dynamic Link won't work.

  2. Create an empty composition from Premiere

    Choose File › Adobe Dynamic Link › New After Effects Composition. The new comp matches the Premiere project's frame size and frame rate and is 30 seconds long by default.

  3. MOGRT: design once, reuse across the team

    Design a graphic in After Effects and export it as a .mogrt. Editors use it in Premiere and change only the fields you exposed, such as names and colours. See MOGRT.

  4. Media Encoder: let it export for you

    Both apps can queue jobs in Adobe Media Encoder, so you can keep working while files export.

Which app for which job

  • Vlogs, interviews, event videos start and finish in Premiere, using ready-made MOGRTs for name straps.
  • Logo intros, film titles, animated infographics build them in After Effects and render or Dynamic Link them in.
  • Removing objects, tracking signs, replacing skies detailed compositing happens in After Effects. For simple cases, try Object Mask in Premiere first.
  • Captions, sound, grading the whole film do these in Premiere, where you see the whole story in order.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Editing a whole film in After EffectsIt isn't built for arranging long footage, so previews get slow and changes get painful. Cut in Premiere and send only effects shots over.
  • Building complex animation with Premiere keyframesIt works up to a point, but when many elements move together After Effects' Graph Editor and text animators give far more control.
  • Dynamic Link not showing upCheck that both apps are the same version, for example 27.0 in both.
  • Rendering an MP4 from After Effects to overlay in PremiereMP4 (H.264) can't store transparency. Use Dynamic Link, or render ProRes 4444 with RGB + Alpha.

Using a version older than 27.0

Premiere Pro 25.x and Premiere 26.x
  • Before 26.0 the app was called Adobe Premiere Pro. Its role and how it works with After Effects are the same.
  • Dynamic Link and MOGRTs work the same way; the two apps' versions still need to match.
