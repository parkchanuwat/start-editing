Markers: pins on your timeline

A marker is a pin on a point in time. Use markers to note what needs fixing, split a video into chapters, or tap out the beat of a song to cut to. One press of M adds one.

  • Premiere 27.0
  • Updated 27 Nov 2026
  • Beginner
  • Based on Adobe Help Center

Keep the Markers panel open. Window › Markers lists every marker; double-click one to see its details. The latest version also shows where the In and Out points fall among the markers.

Clip markers vs sequence markers

Clip markers are added in the Source Monitor and stay with the clip wherever you place it. They are good for flagging the best moments in your footage.

Sequence markers are added in the Program Monitor or on the timeline and sit on the sequence's time. Use them for edit notes and chapters.

Steps

  1. Park the playhead and press M

    Move the playhead where you want the marker, then press M or click Add Marker under the monitor.

    You can press M while a song plays to tap out the beat, then cut clips to the pins. Clips snap to markers.

  2. Double-click a marker to edit it

    Give it a name, add a comment, change its colour or type, and set a Duration if you want a range rather than a single point.

  3. Jump between markers

    Press Shift+M for the next marker and Ctrl+Shift+M / Cmd+Shift+M for the previous one, or click an entry in the Markers panel.

  4. Handle many markers at once

    In the Markers panel, selecting two or more track items shows all their markers together, and you can delete several selected markers in one go.

What markers are good for

  • Client feedback. Drop a Comment at each fix, then work through them with Shift+M.
  • Cutting to music. Mark the beats first, then drag clips so they snap to the pins.
  • YouTube chapters. Copy the times of your Chapter markers into the video description as a list of timestamps (following YouTube's chapter rules). The demo above shows the kind of text you'd paste.
  • Ad breaks. Use Segmentation markers as ranges so whoever receives the file knows where ads can go.

Common beginner mistakes

  • The marker isn't on the timelineIt was added in the Source Monitor, so it became a clip marker on the clip rather than a pin on the sequence.
  • Deleting a section leaves markers behindSequence markers belong to time, not clips. Turn on Ripple Sequence Markers in the Markers menu if you want them to move with ripple edits.
  • Colours mean different things to different peopleAgree on meanings with your team (red to fix, green approved, for example). You can also set default marker colours.
  • Expecting Chapter markers to become YouTube chapters on their ownYouTube builds chapters from timestamps in the description, so you need to paste them in.

Using a version older than 27.0

Premiere 25.x–26.x
  • Adding and editing markers works the same.
  • Older Markers panels may lack filters and won't show In/Out points in the list.
