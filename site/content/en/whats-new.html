What's new in 27.0

Premiere and After Effects 27.0 shipped in November 2026, the big release of the year. The changes beginners will notice most are Color Mode replacing the Lumetri Color panel, the AI Assistant, and higher minimum system requirements.

  • Premiere & After Effects 27.0
  • Updated 27 Nov 2026
  • All levels
  • Based on Adobe Help Center

Before you update: 27.0 needs Windows 11 v24H2 or later, or an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15 or later. See System requirements and install.

Premiere 27.0

FeatureWhat it is
Color ModeA new grading mode in the header bar that replaces the Lumetri Color panel. See Color grading with Color Mode.
AI AssistantA panel where you type requests or ask how to do things; some actions use Firefly generative credits
Generated audioGenerate music, sound effects and soundscapes onto the timeline
Generative Extend for transitionsFills in frames when a clip is too short for a transition
Eight new effectsIncluding Bevel Alpha, Bevel & Emboss, Posterize, Radial Blur and Zoom Blur
UsabilityWaveforms while recording, animated thumbnails in the Effects panel, a card layout in the Properties panel, and a Live Ripple Edit preference
PerformanceAI features run faster on NVIDIA RTX GPUs

After Effects 27.0

FeatureWhat it is

Better Auto-Save recoveryEasier to open auto-saved work after a crash

Editable Illustrator textText from Illustrator files stays editable type in After Effects
New effectsIncluding Contour Paint and De-esser
PNGA compression slider for PNG renders

The 2026 timeline

  • 26.0 (Jan) Premiere Pro renamed Adobe Premiere; Object Mask and built-in Adobe Stock · After Effects can animate variable fonts
  • 26.2 (Apr) Premiere adds Content Credentials at export and a Color Mode public beta in the beta app · After Effects gets Object Matte
  • 26.3 (Jun) Premiere adds 90+ modern effects, transitions and animations · After Effects gets Depth of Field in Advanced 3D
  • 26.5 (Sep) Premiere gets the Generative Media Tool · After Effects redesigns Effect Controls and supports ACES 2.0
  • 27.0 (Nov) The major release in the tables above

Should you update now?

  • You're close to a deadlineFinish in your current version. A project opened and saved in the new version won't open in the old one.
  • You work in a teamAgree to update together, and update Premiere and After Effects as a pair so Dynamic Link keeps working.
  • You rely on plug-insCheck that the developer supports 27.0.
