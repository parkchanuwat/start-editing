MOGRT (Motion Graphics templates)

A .mogrt file is a ready-made, editable motion graphic: a lower third, an opening title, an animated logo. A designer builds it once; editors just change the text, colours or images, so the whole project stays consistent.

  • Premiere 27.0
  • Updated 27 Nov 2026
  • Beginner
  • Based on Adobe Help Center

Can't find the menus from an older tutorial? Older videos use the Browse and Edit tabs of the Essential Graphics panel. Today you find templates in the Graphics Templates panel and edit them in the Properties panel.

Why use MOGRTs

  • Reuse across a whole project. Twenty name straps that all look the same; only the names change.
  • Editors change only what the designer exposed, such as text, colours or animation options. Everything else is locked, so the brand stays intact.
  • Bring in complex After Effects work. Templates built in After Effects can include particles, special effects and 3D, and editors use them in Premiere without opening After Effects themselves.
  • Special kinds exist, such as data-driven templates that build charts from CSV/TSV files, and templates whose images or videos can be swapped (Media Replacement).

Using a template in Premiere

  1. Open the Graphics Templates panel

    Choose Window › Graphics Templates and go to the My Templates tab to see installed templates, including the ones that come with Premiere.

  2. Drag it into the timeline

    Drop the template on a video track above your footage, such as V2.

  3. Edit it in the Properties panel

    Select the template clip in the timeline and look at the Properties panel. You'll see the fields the designer set up: text, colour, animation options, and on some templates fonts, size or faux styles.

  4. Change its length

    Drag the left or right edge of the template clip in the timeline to shorten or lengthen it.

Installing a .mogrt file

  • Click Install Motion Graphics template in the Graphics Templates panel and choose the .mogrt file.
  • Find more templates from Adobe Stock inside the app.
  • To share with a team, right-click a template and choose Copy to Library to keep it in Creative Cloud Libraries.

Making your own in After Effects (overview)

  1. Open the template workspace

    In After Effects, choose Window › Workspace › Essential Graphics and add your main composition to the Essential Graphics panel.

  2. Drag in the properties editors may change

    Drag properties from the timeline into the Essential Graphics panel, such as Source Text or a shape's colour. Everything you add becomes a field editors see in Premiere.

  3. Group and name fields clearly

    Use Add Formatting › Add Group at the lower left of the panel to keep related fields together.

  4. Export

    Export the Motion Graphics template. The images, videos and pre-comps it needs are packaged with it.

Graphics made in Premiere can be exported as .mogrt files too, which suits simple titles you reuse often.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Fonts are missing and the text looks wrongYour computer doesn't have the designer's font. Install or activate it first, especially Thai fonts that don't ship with the system.
  • Can't find the field to changeYou can only edit what the designer exposed. Anything else has to be changed in After Effects.
  • An After Effects template plays back choppyComplex templates are heavy to process. Render that section before reviewing.
  • Stretching it throws off the in and out animationsIf the designer used Responsive Design, the intro and outro keep their timing when you stretch it. If not, keep close to the original length.

Using an older version

Premiere versions with the Essential Graphics panel
  • Older versions use the Browse tab to find templates and the Edit tab to change them. The workflow is the same; only the menus moved.
  • The Legacy Titler has been retired; upgrade old titles to Source Graphics.
